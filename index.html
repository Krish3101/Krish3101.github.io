About

I am a Computer Science Engineering student at MIT ADT University, Pune, who prefers systems whose behaviour you can reason about on paper. Most of my work sits behind the API line: rule engines, ledgers, ingestion pipelines, and the schemas that keep them honest.

My habit across every repository here is the same — write the deterministic core first, put it under test, and only then attach the transport, the UI, or the model. It is why the AI projects fall back gracefully when a key is missing and why the geospatial pipeline does its maths in PostGIS rather than in Python.
